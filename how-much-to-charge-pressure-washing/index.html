How Much Should You Charge for Pressure Washing?

There is no single price that fits every pressure washing business or every job. A more durable approach is to calculate your own job cost and then choose a target margin.

Start with labor

Estimate the time and number of workers required.

Add the job costs

Include chemicals, materials, fuel, travel and other expenses.

Cover overhead

Allocate a reasonable share of equipment, insurance, software, administration and other operating costs.

Set your target margin

Use a margin appropriate for your business and then review the resulting price against job conditions and local market realities.
