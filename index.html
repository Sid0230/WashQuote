Pressure washing pricing calculator FAQ

How should I price a pressure washing job?

Start with your complete job cost, set your target profit margin, and use the result as your starting quote. Always consider local market conditions, job difficulty and your minimum charge.

What does WashQuote calculate?

WashQuote factors in labor, materials, travel, other job costs, overhead, target margin and applicable fees to help you create a customer-ready quote.

Can I use WashQuote for different pressure washing services?

Yes. Use it for driveways, house washing, roof washing, decks/patios, commercial work and other services.

Is WashQuote free?

WashQuote is a paid product. Starter is currently being validated at $9/month or $79/year, while Pro adds customer management, repeat quoting, quote pipeline and business analytics.

What is the difference between Starter and Pro?

Starter = calculate, quote and send. Pro = manage customers, repeat work and the business behind the quotes.

Does WashQuote guarantee the right price?

No. It is a pricing and planning tool. Your final price should account for your local market, site conditions, regulations and actual business costs.
