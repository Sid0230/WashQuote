What should a pressure washing quote include?

Start with direct labor and materials. Add travel or fuel, a fair share of overhead, and any applicable taxes or payment fees. Then set the profit margin your business needs.

Why calculate cost before price?

A customer price that looks competitive can still lose money if labor, chemicals, travel and overhead are not included. A cost-first calculator makes those inputs visible before you send the quote.
